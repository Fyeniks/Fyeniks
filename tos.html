Commission rules

Terms of Service

These terms explain how commissions work, what I need from you, and what you can expect during the editing process.

01

General

By ordering an edit, you agree to these terms. I can refuse projects that do not fit my style, schedule, or boundaries.

02

Payment

Prices depend on the client, edit type, footage length, deadlines, and the amount of work required. Payment details are agreed before work starts. Payments via Stripe

03

Materials

Please send footage, links, timestamps, references, and any important notes. I can also download Twitch VODs or clips myself if you provide the links.

04

Revisions

Small revisions are included when they match the original request. Major changes, new concepts, or extra work may cost more.

05

Delivery

Delivery time depends on the project size and current queue. Final files are delivered in a format suitable for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or your requested platform.

06

Usage

You can post the finished edit on your channels. I may showcase the work in my portfolio unless we agree otherwise before the project starts.

07

Cancellations

If a project is cancelled after work has started, the finished part or time already spent may still be charged.

08

Contact

Have questions before ordering? Message me first and I will confirm the scope, price, and timeline before starting.
