FAQ

Quick answers before ordering an edit.

01

What videos do you edit?

I edit YouTube videos, stream highlights, shorts, TikToks, reels, and creator-focused gaming content.

02

What programs do you use?

I use Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe After Effects for editing, motion, effects, and final polish.

03

What do I need to send?

Send the footage, links, timestamps if you have them, style references, and where the final video will be posted. I can also download videos from Twitch myself.

04

What are the prices?

Prices vary depending on the client, the type of edit, and the amount of work required for the project.
