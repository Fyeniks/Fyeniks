FAQ

Quick answers before ordering an edit

01

What videos do you edit?

I edit YouTube videos, stream highlights, shorts, TikToks, reels, and creator-focused gaming content.

02

What programs do you use?

I use Adobe software for editing, motion, effects, and final polish.

03

What do I need to send?

Send the footage, timestamps, links, channel emotes, and any other materials you'd like included in the edit. I can download VODs directly from Twitch. If timestamps are not provided, an additional fee will apply for reviewing the VOD.

04

What is your turnaround time?

Shorts usually take 1–2 days, and stream highlights take 2–4 days. It all depends on the project's complexity, my current workload, and your place in the queue. If you have a tight deadline, let me know and we can work it out!
